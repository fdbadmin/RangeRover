RangeRover — User Manual

1) Introduction

RangeRover is a Shell‑styled probabilistic volumetric screener for STOIIP (oil) and GIIP (gas). It runs Monte Carlo simulations using user‑defined distributions (Normal, Triangular, PERT, Uniform, Known, Discrete) for the key volumetric inputs and an optional Recovery Factor, producing CDFs, histograms, a tornado sensitivity chart, and CSV exports. Oil uses Bo (RB/STB); Gas uses Bg (scf/SCF). Recovery Factor multiplies volumes directly.

2) Quick start

  1. Select Fluid: Oil, Gas, Coal Seam Gas, Oil + Solution Gas, or Gas + Vaporized Oil (units & formula update automatically).
  2. Choose units for Area and Thickness.
  3. For each parameter (Area, Thickness, NTG, Porosity, Water Saturation, Bo/Bg), choose a Distribution (Normal, Triangular, PERT, Uniform, Known, Discrete) and provide its parameters. Small labels above each box guide what to enter (e.g., Mean/Std Dev or Min/Mode/Max).
  4. Set the Recovery Factor distribution if needed.
  5. Press Run Simulation. Use the Reset buttons to re-zoom plots.

3) Inputs & units

  • Area: m², km², acres, ft² (internally converted to acres).
  • Thickness: m or ft (internally converted to ft).
  • NTG, Porosity, Water Saturation are treated as fractions (0–1). Previews display in % for readability.
  • Bo (RB/STB) for Oil; Bg (scf/SCF) for Gas; Rs (scf/bbl) with Oil + Solution Gas; Rv (bbl/scf) with Gas + Vaporized Oil.
  • Coal Seam Gas uses Coal Density (g/cm³, typically 1.3–1.5) and Gas Content (m³/ton) instead of porosity/saturation parameters.

4) Distributions

Supported distributions: Normal (mean, std dev), Triangular (min, mode, max), PERT (min, mode, max), Uniform (min, max), Known (P90, P50, P10), and Discrete (1–3 values with optional weights).

Discrete flexibility: you may enter one, two, or three values. Weights are optional. If left blank, weights auto balance (two values → 50/50; three values → ~33/33/33). Any provided weights are normalized automatically.
PERT: Smooth alternative to Triangular using a Beta‑PERT (λ = 4). Inputs: Min, Mode, Max. Mode is used for deterministic and tornado base cases; Min/Max are used for tornado low/high. For Discrete, the deterministic mid‑case uses the highest‑weighted value (if weights are blank, values are treated as equal). The implied mean is (a + λ·m + b)/(λ+2). Sampling is truncated to physical bounds for each parameter.

5) Physical truncation

All random draws are constrained to physically sensible ranges:

  • Fractions (NTG, φ, Sw, RF): 0 to 1.
  • Bo ≥ 1; Bg > 0; Area/Thickness > 0.

Normal draws use truncated sampling (accept–reject) to avoid piling mass at the bounds.

6) Monte Carlo engine

RangeRover samples each input according to its distribution and computes in-place volumes:

  • Oil (STOIIP): 7,758 × A × h × NTG × φ × (1 − Sw) / Bo (Bo in RB/STB) → plots in MMBO
  • Gas (GIIP): 43,560 × A × h × NTG × φ × (1 − Sw) / Bg (Bg in scf/SCF) → plots in BCF
  • Solution gas (GIIP from oil): 7,758 × A × h × NTG × φ × (1 − Sw) × Rs / Bo (SCF) → plots in BCF
  • Total (Oil + Solution Gas, MMBOE): STOIIP (MMBO) + GIIPsg (MMBOE), where GIIPsg (MMBOE) = SCF / 5.8e9 (equivalently BCF / 5.8).
  • Vaporized oil from gas: 43,560 × A × h × NTG × φ × (1 − Sw) × Rv / Bg (STB) → plots in MMBO
  • Total (Gas + Vaporized Oil, MMBOE): GIIP (MMBOE) + Vaporized oil (MMBO), where GIIP (MMBOE) = SCF / 5.8e9 (equivalently BCF / 5.8).

Recoverable volume = In-place × RF. P10/P50/P90 are computed from the empirical CDF of simulated samples. Deterministic mid-case uses the most likely input values (Normal→mean; Triangular/PERT→mode; Uniform→midpoint; Known→P50; Discrete→highest‑weighted value — equal weights if blank).

7) Outputs

  • Results metric (Oil + Solution Gas or Gas + Vaporized Oil):
    • Oil + Solution Gas: STOIIP (MMBO), GIIP (BCF), or Total (MMBOE)
    • Gas + Vaporized Oil: GIIP (BCF), Vaporized Oil (MMBO), or Total (MMBOE)
    All plots, the tornado axis, and the results table follow the selected unit.
  • In‑place & Recoverable CDFs with red markers at P90/P50/P10 and a purple diamond for the deterministic mid‑case.
  • Histograms with vertical markers for P90/P50/P10 and deterministic mid‑case.
  • Tornado sensitivity shows impact of low/high changes in each input vs deterministic mid‑case. Green = upside, Red = downside.
  • Summary table shows P90/P50/P10 and Deterministic Mid Case (with P‑value, e.g., P45).
  • CSV exports: Summary CSV includes P90/P50/P10 and deterministic values. Samples CSV includes every iteration with all inputs (Area, Thickness, NtG, Porosity, Sw, Bo/Bg, RF) plus in‑place and recoverable volumes, with units.

8) Tornado sensitivity

Computed by changing one parameter at a time from its “low” to “high” while others are fixed at base (deterministic mid‑case; weight‑aware for Discrete). Low/High are taken from distribution definitions: Normal = mean±1.28σ; Triangular/PERT/Uniform = Min/Max; Known = P90/P10; Discrete = min/max of values. Axis units follow the selected metric (MMBO, BCF, or MMBOE). Bars are:

  • Green for positive Δ (upside),
  • Red for negative Δ (downside).

Sorted from biggest impact (top) to smallest (bottom). Left aligned, narrower for readability.

9) CSV export

Use the buttons to export a summary CSV and a samples CSV (in-place & recoverable per iteration).

10) Tips

  • Set a seed for reproducible results.
  • Increase iterations for smoother CDFs.
  • Use PERT instead of Triangular for a smoother shape when you know min/mode/max.
  • Use Normal (default) unless you have clear min/mode/max or P90/P50/P10 knowledge.

11) Troubleshooting

  • Input error messages point to the first offending field (e.g., Triangular min ≤ mode ≤ max).
  • If a plot appears blank, check for missing numbers in required fields.

12) Glossary

  • P10/P50/P90: high/median/low outcomes (90/50/10% probability of being exceeded).
  • NTG: Net to Gross. φ: Porosity. Sw: Water saturation.
  • PERT: Beta‑PERT distribution (λ=4) defined by Min, Mode, Max; smoother alternative to Triangular.
  • Bg: Gas formation volume factor in scf/SCF (reservoir cubic feet per standard cubic foot).
  • Rs: Solution gas‑oil ratio in scf/bbl.
  • Rv: Vaporized oil‑gas ratio in bbl/scf.
